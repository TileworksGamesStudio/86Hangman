HOUSE RULES

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Uncover the Secret Bar Term: Tap letters on the virtual keyboard or type with physical keys to reveal letters in the cocktail, spirit, or technique.
  2. Manage Your Strikes: You are permitted 6 incorrect letter guesses. Each mistake assembles part of the shaker apparatus.
  3. Avoid Getting 86’d: If you accrue 6 strikes before completing the term, the pour is lost to the spill mat.
  4. Spaces & Punctuation: Spaces and symbols (hyphens, apostrophes) are automatically revealed.
  5. Midnight Release: A new cocktail challenge drops every midnight. Past released editions are archived in The Cellar Vault.
